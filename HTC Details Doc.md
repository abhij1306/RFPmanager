# **About HTC Global Pty Ltd**

HTC Global Pty Ltd is an Australian technology consulting and services company specializing in IT Service Management (ITSM), Digital Transformation, Enterprise Integrations, Managed Services, and Business Technology Solutions.

As a Certified Freshworks Partner in the ANZ region, HTC Global helps organizations modernize service delivery, streamline operations, automate workflows, and improve user experiences through scalable, cloud-based solutions aligned with industry best practices.

We provide end-to-end consulting, implementation, integration, training, support, and managed services to public and private sector organizations across Australia.
