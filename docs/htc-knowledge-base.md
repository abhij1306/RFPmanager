## 1. Company Overview

HTC Global Pty Ltd is an Australian-owned ICT consulting and digital solutions provider headquartered in Bella Vista, New South Wales. It helps government agencies, educational institutions, and private-sector organisations accelerate digital transformation through scalable, business-focused technology solutions that improve operational efficiency, service delivery, and customer experience.

The team provides end-to-end consulting, implementation, integration, development, managed services, and ongoing operational support. Technology expertise spans Freshworks, Zendesk, Microsoft technologies, cloud platforms, CRM solutions, digital experience platforms, workflow automation, and custom software development.

## 5. Indicative Implementation Timeline (typical 6-week, medium-sized project)

| Week | Phase | Key Activities |

| 2 | Solution Design & Core Configuration | Org structure/users/roles/permissions configured; core platform settings & security; business rules, notifications, communication channels; foundational components per approved design |

| 4 | Integrations & Advanced Configuration | Third-party/enterprise integrations configured; advanced workflows/automation completed; APIs/data sync/migration (where applicable); internal QA & config review |

| 6 | Go-Live & Hypercare | Production deployment & go-live; post-deployment validation; performance/adoption monitoring; hypercare support; project closure & BAU transition |

**Notes:** Timeline is indicative; adjusts for project size, complexity, integrations, data migration, custom development. Extensive integrations/custom development/phased rollouts may add stages. Iterative delivery with regular stakeholder reviews.
