# 1  Company Overview

## 1.1  About HTC Global

HTC Global Pty Ltd is an Australian-owned ICT consulting and digital solutions provider headquartered in Norwest, New South Wales. We help government agencies, educational institutions, and private sector organisations accelerate their digital transformation by delivering innovative, scalable, and business-focused technology solutions that improve operational efficiency, service delivery, and customer experience.

At HTC Global, we believe technology should enable better business outcomes.
